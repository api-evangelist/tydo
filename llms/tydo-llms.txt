# Tydo

> Tydo provides autonomous audits for Shopify stores, delivered inside Claude. One integration runs hundreds of audits — retention, shipping, email & SMS, and more — scoring a store against similar brands, tracking trends on a cadence, and routing every finding to a fix: do it yourself, turn on an agent, or get matched to a vetted partner via dtcmvp. Backed by Greylock. Currently waitlist-only, with no public developer portal, API documentation, or published MCP endpoint.
